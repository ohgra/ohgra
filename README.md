## About Me

I am a PhD researcher in the **Department of Electrical Engineering, IIT Delhi**, working with
**Prof. Tanmoy Chakraborty**. My research asks a simple question with hard answers:
*when a language model says something, how much does it believe it, how good was the reasoning
behind it, and can a human trust the verdict?*

My thesis, **Explainable and Safe AI for Human-Centric Claim Verification**, sits at the
intersection of NLP, LLM reasoning, uncertainty estimation and the psychology of misinformation.
Before the PhD I trained as a statistician at **ISI Kolkata** and worked on risk models and
explainable AI in banking and finance, which is why I care about models that can be measured,
audited and explained.

## Research Areas

<table>
<tr>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**LLM Reasoning and Evaluation**<br/>
Assessing the quality of offline reasoning traces in language models with Markovian, ODE-guided scoring.

**Model Belief and Uncertainty**<br/>
Going beyond token logits to measure what a model actually believes, using distribution-based plausibility.

**Explainable and Trustworthy AI**<br/>
Interpretable, auditable systems, from SHAP-based risk models in banking to explainable fact-checking.

</td>
<td width="50%" valign="top">

**Misinformation and Claim Verification**<br/>
Human-centric fact-checking that accounts for cognitive bias and the psychology of belief.

**Multimodal Content Verification**<br/>
Fusion architectures for verifying mixed-source text and image content.

**Probabilistic and Statistical Modeling**<br/>
Stochastic processes, Markov models and multivariate analysis as tools for principled ML.

</td>
</tr>
